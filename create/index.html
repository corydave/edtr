        About EDTR Studio

        Every EDTR app in one window. Each app is the very same app you can open on its own; the Studio just keeps them side by side, so switching is one click and nothing closes behind you.

        Keyboard

        Ctrl+Shift+1 ... 9Switch to the first ... ninth app in the rail (works inside an app too, unless that app uses the key itself)
        Ctrl+Shift+0Back to the rail (on a phone: the Apps sheet)
        Up / DownMove along the rail; Enter opens
        Shift+F10 or right-clickAn app's menu: open it in its own tab, reload it, or close it

        Good to know

        • Apps you aren't looking at keep running, so memory adds up: close the big ones (VIDEDTR, 3DEDTR) when you're done.
        • Each app saves, exports and keeps its own crash backups exactly as it does on its own.
        • Install the Studio and it gets its own window and icon; it works offline once each app has been opened once.
