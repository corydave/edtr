WHTBRDR

Privacy

For the WHTBRDR app (edtrapps.com/whtbrdr) and the WHTBRDR Chrome extension. Last updated 22 October 2026.

In short: nothing you draw, record or type leaves your computer. WHTBRDR has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics and no ads. It never sells, shares or sends your data anywhere.

The Chrome extension

  • It only works where you ask it to. It uses Chrome's activeTab permission: it can't see or change any page until you click its button on that tab. It does not ask to read your data on all websites.
  • Your drawings live in the page's memory and are gone when you reload or close the page.
  • Exports (PNG and PDF) are made on your computer and saved through Chrome's own Save dialog.
  • Recordings of a tab are made on your computer, in the extension's own hidden page, and saved straight to your Downloads folder. The camera and microphone are only on while you record (or while the Record panel shows your camera bubble), and only once you have allowed them on the extension's setup page. The web page you are recording can't see your camera or hear your microphone.
  • What is stored: only the toolbar's own settings (where it sits, whether clicks show, and whether the camera and microphone are on for recording), in Chrome's storage for the extension on this computer. While a recording is running, which tab it is (forgotten when Chrome closes). Never your drawings, recordings or anything about the pages you visit.
  • Nothing is sent over the internet. The extension makes no network requests. Its fonts are built in.

The WHTBRDR app

  • It runs in your browser. Your boards, PDFs and pictures stay on your computer; nothing is uploaded.
  • Your settings, and a backup of the board you are working on (so a crash doesn't lose it), are kept in your browser's storage on this computer. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.
  • When you open a PDF, the online edition of the app may fetch its PDF reader (pdf.js) from jsDelivr. That request carries no information about you or your files. The offline edition has it built in.

Children and schools

Because WHTBRDR collects no personal information, it collects none from children either. It is made for classrooms, and nothing in it needs a sign-in.
