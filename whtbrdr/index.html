Draw with a mouse, a stylus or a finger. The arrow keys slide the board; Ctrl plus and Ctrl minus zoom; Ctrl 0 fits the page. Hold Space and drag to slide it with the mouse.

    Left and Right go to the page before or after; with Shift they pick the pages on the way. Alt with Left or Right moves the picked pages. Delete removes them. Enter goes back to the board.
