SNDEDTR

A multitrack audio editor that runs entirely in this tab. Files you open are decoded in the browser and never sent anywhere; there is no server, no account, and no analytics.

Installing it. Served over https this installs as an app: use the install button in the top bar, the one in Chrome or Edge's address bar, or Share then Add to Home Screen on iOS. It then opens in its own window and works with no network at all. Once installed, audio files can be opened straight into SNDEDTR from your desktop.

Split, Trim and Delete act on the selected track - the one highlighted on the left. Click a lane to select it.
