Screen and webcam recorder. Everything stays on this machine.

AUDIO

Heads up: this mode writes a single video file, so your voice and the screen's audio are permanently mixed into one track. Ticking the separate mic file above keeps a clean copy of your voice.

LOGO

Drawn onto the recording, so this works in Combined mode only - the other modes record your screen as-is, with no frame of ours to paint on.

SAVE
Files will download to your Downloads folder.

Choosing a folder streams each recording straight to disk as it goes, instead of holding it in memory until you stop. For anything longer than a few minutes, choose a folder.

2. Check your framing, then record

Drag the bubble to move it. Drag the blue handle to resize. Keyboard: 1-6 hot spots (these also drop you out of full-frame), 0 full-frame webcam, c confetti, l countdown, f fireworks, h hearts, b blank, k webcam out of the recording, r reset position.

This is only to check your framing and lighting. The webcam records as its own full-frame file, so you will place and shape it in your editor.

    A screen and webcam recorder that runs entirely in your browser.
         Nothing is uploaded, there is no server, and no analytics - recordings
         are written straight to your disk.
    

      
    The three modes
    
      
      
        
    • Separate files - a clean screen recording plus a
            full-frame webcam recording. Two files, nothing baked in. Place the
            webcam in your editor.
      • 
        
    • Combined - one finished file with your face already
            in it. Two ways of getting it there, below.
      • 
        
    • Screen only - screen and narration, no camera.
      • 
      
    

      
    Combined: how your face gets in
    
      
      
        
    • Composite it in - drawn onto the video as a circle,
            rounded rectangle or square. Any shape, positioned from the preview.
            Adjustable while recording, but only while you can see this window.
      • 
        
    • Pop-out only - no compositing. The floating camera
            window is physically on your screen, so the capture records it.
            Rectangular, but full quality and draggable the whole time.
      • 
      
    

      
    What you share matters - and it flips
    
      
      
        
    • Every mode except pop-out: share a single window
            so this app stays out of the shot.
      • 
        
    • Pop-out mode is the opposite - it needs a
            whole screen. Share one window and the floating
            camera is not captured, so your face will be missing entirely.
      • 
      
    

      
    Audio
    
      
      
        
    • Separate files keep your voice in the webcam file and system audio in
            the screen file, so they stay separable.
      • 
        
    • Single-file modes have one audio track, so voice and system audio are
            mixed permanently. Tick save my mic as its own file to keep
            a clean copy of your voice.
      • 
      
    

      
    Keyboard
    
      
      
        
    • 1-6 jump the webcam to a hot spot (Composite
            mode) - the numbered dots on the preview. It glides rather than jumps,
            so the move looks deliberate in the recording.
      • 
        
    • r resets the webcam to its default position - the escape
            hatch if it ever ends up somewhere you cannot reach.
      • 
        
    • l countdown, c confetti, f fireworks,
            h hearts,
            b blanks the screen, k keeps your webcam out
            of the recording, 0 full-frame webcam. Press ?
            anywhere for the full list.
      • 
        
    • Webcam overlay focused: arrows move it,
            + / - resize, hold Shift for bigger steps.
      • 
        
    • Esc closes this dialog.
      • 
      
    

      
    Overlay controls (Composite mode)
    
      
      
        
    • Blank screen (b) - hides your screen from
            the recording without stopping it. Your face, watermark and timer stay
            visible, so you can keep talking while you deal with whatever
            appeared. It always resets between takes.
      • 
        
    • Logo - a watermark drawn onto the recording. Choose a
            text badge, one of the two built-in marks, or upload your own image.
            An uploaded image is kept in this browser on this machine and is
            never sent anywhere. It is drawn after the webcam, so it
            stays visible when you cut to full frame.
      • 
        
    • Keep the webcam out of the recording
            (k, the circle-and-slash) - the mirror of Blank screen.
            Your camera keeps running and you keep seeing yourself;
            the recording simply stops including you. The overlay goes dashed and
            says not recorded, because this is the one place where what
            you see is deliberately not what you get. The pop-out window keeps
            working too. It is not a substitute for choosing Screen only
            up front, which never asks for the camera at all.
      • 
        
    • Full-frame webcam (0) - the webcam zooms
            up to fill the whole frame and back again. The zoom itself is recorded,
            so it reads as an intentional cut to camera.
      • 
        
    • Confetti (c) - a burst that falls through
            the frame and clears itself. Costs nothing when idle.
      • 
        
    • Fireworks (f) - five staggered shells that
            climb and burst. Hearts (h) float up from
            the bottom. Both clear themselves the same way.
      • 
        
    • Countdown (l) - the cinema academy leader:
            half a second of flat black, then crosshairs, a ringed circle, a hand
            sweeping once per numeral, and 3-2-1. 2.9 seconds in all. Unlike the
            others it is opaque - covering the frame is what a leader is
            for, and it is what makes it read as deliberate rather than as an
            overlay that leaked in. Talk over it. The black at the front is
            deliberate breathing room for the edit: a frame with nothing on it is
            easy to find when scrubbing and gives you somewhere to cut.
      • 
        
    • Show timer on screen - burns the elapsed time into the
            recording as a pill in the top-left. Off by default.
            Your own timer in the control bar is always there regardless, and both
            read the same clock, so both stop when you pause.
      • 
      
    

      
    Adding your own sounds
    
      
      
        
    • Drop a file in this folder named sfx-applause,
            sfx-trombone, sfx-celebrate,
            sfx-laugh or sfx-rimshot, with any of
            .mp3 .wav .m4a .ogg.
            No renaming needed.
      • 
        
    • A file always beats the synthesized version, and
            .mp3 is tried first - so dropping in an mp3 overrides a
            wav of the same name without deleting anything.
      • 
        
    • Hover a sound button to see which you are hearing: a synthesized
            fallback says so in its tooltip.
      • 
        
    • Reload after adding a file; sounds are loaded once at startup.

    Pause
    
      
      
        
    • Pause genuinely cuts the paused span out - the file
            is shorter, not padded with a frozen frame. Works in every mode.
      • 
        
    • Compositing stops while paused, so it costs nothing to leave paused.
      • 
      
    

      
    Installing
    
      
      
        
    • Use the Install button in the header, or Chrome's own
            install icon in the address bar. It then opens in its own window with
            no browser chrome - which also keeps that chrome out of your
            recordings.
      • 
        
    • It launches offline. Web fonts are the one thing not cached, so
            offline the type falls back to your system fonts. Nothing breaks.
      • 
      
    

      
    Requirements
    
      
      
        
    • Chrome or Edge, served over https or from localhost.
      • 
        
    • System audio capture needs Chrome 141+ on macOS 14.2+.
      • 
        
    • Choosing a save folder streams to disk as you record - worth doing
            for anything longer than a few minutes.
