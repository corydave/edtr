          Your work is safe

          Every change can be undone (Ctrl+Z). Save puts your whole game in one .gamedtr file you can keep, email or open on another computer. If the page closes before you save, GAMEDTR offers your work back the next time it opens.

          GAMEDTR is a 2D game maker for kids and teens. Make a platformer, a maze or an arcade game: paint levels, draw your own pixel art, snap blocks together to say what things do, and make chiptune sounds and tunes. Then export it as one web page that anyone can play, even offline, on a computer, tablet or phone.

          Your privacy

          • No account, no tracking, no ads, no chat and no public gallery.
          • Your games never leave this device unless you save or share a file yourself.
          • The crash backup is kept in this browser only.
          • The only thing GAMEDTR ever asks the internet is "is there a newer version?", from its own website.
