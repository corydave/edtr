About this lab

A test page for recording with TELEDTR on a folding phone: the script near the camera, the controls under your thumbs, and the camera recording what you see in the preview. Recordings stay on this device: Save puts the video (and its captions) in your Downloads.

Keys: R records or stops, Space starts or pauses the script, Up and Down change the pace, + and - the text size, Home goes to the start.

The captions are timed from the script as it passed the reading line, so they follow the prompter, not your voice: a good first draft.
