    In the prompter, the keys work the same as a presentation clicker: most clickers send the arrow and Page keys.

    Write

    Start a line with # for a section heading (## for a smaller one), or use the heading tool at the top of the rail. Put **bold** and *italic* around words to stress while you read (or the B and I tools).

    Cue marks

    Cue marks are in square brackets. The prompter shows them and never reads them aloud. [PAUSE] holds the text for a moment, [PAUSE 3s] holds it for three seconds, and [SLIDE 2] or [CLICK] reminds you to change a slide. [NOTE: anything] is a note to yourself.

    Time

    The estimate counts your words at the pace in the Inspector (145 words a minute to start), plus each pause. Change the pace and every time updates at once. Headings are not counted, because they are titles, not speech.

    Check

    The Inspector's Script check tab looks the script over as you write: sentences too long for one breath, cue marks with a typo such as [PASUE], bold or italic left open, a missing ], doubled words, and sections much longer than the rest. Select one to go to it; some have a Fix button. Ctrl+F finds and replaces.

    Sections

    The timeline under the script has a block for each section, as wide as its time, so the long ones stand out. The block your cursor is in is highlighted, with a bar for how far in you are. Click a block to go there. Drag it to move it (with a finger, by its dotted grip), or use Alt+Left and Alt+Right. Right-click (or long-press) a block for its menu.

    Prompt

    Press Ctrl+P or the Prompt button. The prompter starts a countdown, then the text moves up to the reading line. Lines you have read are dimmed, and the bar at the bottom shows the section you are in. The prompter remembers where you stopped, and keeps the screen awake while it is open.

    Change things while prompting

    The pace and the text size have their own - and + buttons in the prompter's controls (hold one to keep going), as do the keys: Up and Down for the pace while the text moves, + and - for the size. The sliders button opens the Inspector beside the text, with every setting. F switches full screen, and a right-click or long-press on the text opens a menu. On a touch screen, when the controls have faded, the first tap brings them back without pausing.

    Movement

    In the Inspector's Pace section: Smooth (the text glides up steadily, the default), A line at a time (each line holds still, then moves up) or A page at a time (the text stays still while you read down the screen, then turns).

    Files. Save writes a .teledtr file. Open reads one, or a plain text or Markdown file; you can also drop one anywhere on TELEDTR. Installed as an app, TELEDTR opens .teledtr files from your desktop.
